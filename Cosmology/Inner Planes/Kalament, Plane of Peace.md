# Overview
[[Kalament, Plane of Peace|Kalament]] is an endless, still ocean of light grey smoke. Sailing on the seas is impossible with regular means as anything solid will simply pass straight through. Travel below the surface also proves a challenge, due to the immense depth and pressure. Magical means have been developed to allow objects to float on the surface, though such vessels are difficult and expensive to make.

Air travel is also possible, but made difficult due to the white mist that permeates the plane. The skies are constantly covered by a blanket of mist that gives the plane an ethereal look, and reducing visibility significantly. The thickness of the mist changes, sometimes without warning. One moment visibility could be a few kilometres, the next you could be flying with only a few meters of visibility. The mists dampen all magic, inhibiting magical communication while inside of them. Sound is also dampened, making verbal communication difficult even if you are standing right next to someone.

Sporadic islands float lazily along the smoky surface. Inhabitants of the plane have found ways to use smaller islands as transportation, attaching arcane engines that propel them at surprising speeds through the mists. Larger islands are sometimes tied together, to form floating settlements. The islands can't take much weight, so construction material must be light.
# Environment
## Blazing Light
The mists that permeate the plane protects the surface from a blazing light above. Sometimes, when the mists clear, the light can reach the surface in a fury. It burns away at the smoky oceans, creating pockets of nothing, threatening to plunge unsuspecting travellers into the depths.
