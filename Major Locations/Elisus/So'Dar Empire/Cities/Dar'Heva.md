# Overview
Dar'Heva or true home in [[Soldovin]] is the capital city of [[So'Dar Empire Overview\| So'Dar]]. It is the newest city in the nation being the only city built by the Soldovian people after the House Wars. This city is built with little in mind for beauty. Its wall stand unbroken and untested. Located in by the Glacier Bay. It is known buy outsiders as the *"City of Frozen Tears"* and the *"Home of the Lost"*. 
# History
## Formation
The city that was built in 1340 APC after the House Wars that weakened So'Dar significantly this new city was where House Kin'Dirth'Al consolidated their power and created the power structure that runs the So'Dar Empire
## House Kin'Dirth'Al
After the civil war House Kin'Dirth'Al wanted to show that they were the new central power of the nation and started building Dar'Heva moving away from there past and the old capital [[Kou'Thar]] and making the new Capital at the center of their family estate by the Glacier Bay converting the town of Al'Heva to the city it is today.
