# Overview
A species of birds originating from [[Kalament, Plane of Peace|Kalament]]. They have an innate ability to dampen any magical abilities in an area around them. They are rare, only found on some few larger islands, and are very sought after off-plane.
# Origin
The [[Samsier]] originate from [[Kalament, Plane of Peace|Kalament]]. They are believed to be an original creation of [[Irellia - Mistress of the Tranquil Lake|Irellia]], as no evidence of an evolutionary branch have been found. It is believed that she created them to tame the wild branches of magic, as well as a way to spread the planes calming influence to other planes.
# Climate
The species thrives in temperate climates, though they can survive in warmer climates. They are sensitive to cold.
