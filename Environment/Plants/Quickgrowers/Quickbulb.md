# Overview
The Quickbulb is a fast-growing plant with a lifespan of only a few weeks. During this time, the plant will grow a single stem with a small bulb at the very top. When the plant detects strong movement, the bulb will burst and release spores into the air. The hope is that it bursts during strong winds, spreading spores over a wide area.
# Environments
Has been found growing in [[Storms Reach Overview|Storms Reach]] and along the coastal cliffs of the [[Ohkyr Dominion Overview|Ohkyr Dominion]].
