# Overview
Small spike made from [[Sesmite]]. It weakens the connection to the magical weave when inserted into a living body. Due to their nature, the body naturally rejects the spike, causing it to be ejected after a while. A pure [[Sesmite]] spike will never stay inside a body unless attached in some other way.
# History
[[Sesmite]] spikes were another invention made by the [[Turochan Kingdom Overview|Turochan Kingdom]] during its early days, as a temporary solution for dealing with magic users.
# Usage
Spike into a body. Certain points on the body give greater effect, but may be more difficult to attach and expel faster.
