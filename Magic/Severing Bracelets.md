# Overview
A pair of bracelets that supress magic. A bracelet is in two parts, a thin metal rod made from [[Sesmite]], and a ring made from interlocking metal parts.
# History
The bracelets were first developed by an organization from the [[Turochan Kingdom Overview|Turochan Kingdom]] for use against dangerous magic users. Use has since spread over the continent for depowering dangerous criminals.
# Usage
To attach the bracelets to the victim, the small rods have to first be inserted between the Radius and the Ulna on both arms. Then, the main body of the bracelets clamps onto the rods, locking them in place. The locks on the bracelets are magical in nature, and only a magic user can lock and unlock them.
